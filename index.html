Vibe Coding: Building Software by Collaborating with AI

A hands-on introduction to building software by working with an AI coding partner — how to prompt it, how to read what it hands back, and how to know when the output deserves your trust. Built for a room that mixes engineers with people who've never opened a terminal.

Format: half-day, 4 modules Audience: mixed technical / non-technical Archetype: flat-file lesson site

Before you start

Never paste secrets, credentials, customer data, or anything covered by an NDA into an AI tool. Read the Responsible Use policy before Module 1 — it's short.
