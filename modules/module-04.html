Overview & Objectives

This capstone breaks from the six-part module shape used in Modules 1–3. Instead of separate activity, simulation, and review blocks, it's one extended working session — the deliverable is the assessment.

By the end of this module, you'll be able to:

  • Scope a small, real deliverable to something buildable in the session
  • Run the full loop — prompt, review, judge, iterate — start to finish, unassisted
  • Explain your own verification decisions to a peer, and take critique on them

Prerequisites: Modules 01–03.

2

Capstone Session

Budget this as one continuous block, not four separate timers. A facilitator note on pacing lives in the Teaching Guide.

1 · Scope (10 min)

Pick a small real task from your own work. Write down what "done" looks like before you open the AI tool — this is Module 2's success-criteria skill, applied for real.

2 · Build (35 min)

Run the loop yourself: prompt, read the output like a reviewer, iterate. Apply the blast-radius checklist from Module 3 to decide how much verification your task actually needs.

3 · Teach a slice (10 min, pairs)

Explain one decision you made — a prompt you wrote, a verification step you did or skipped, an iteration you needed — to a partner as if they're about to do the same task.

4 · Critique (15 min, pairs)

Your partner reviews your result the way Module 2 taught: what changed, does it match your stated success criteria, any red flags. They write two critique notes.

Rubric

A capstone is "done" when: the success criteria were written down before building started, the loop was visibly run more than once (no first-draft-and-ship), the verification effort matched the stated blast radius, and the builder can explain why — not just that — each decision was made.
