Responsible Use Policy

Short on purpose. Everyone in the room reads this before the first module starts.

1

Never paste these into an AI tool

  • Passwords, API keys, access tokens, or any other credential
  • Customer data, employee data, or anything else covered by privacy policy or regulation
  • Anything covered by an NDA or marked confidential
  • Source code your employer has told you not to share externally
Why this matters

Most AI tools are external services. Treat a prompt the way you'd treat an email to an outside vendor — appropriate for a public code sample, inappropriate for a credential or a customer record. If you're not sure whether something is sensitive, don't paste it — ask first.

2

Before anything ships

  • A human reviews it — see Module 03's blast-radius checklist for how much review is enough.
  • You know where any third-party code or libraries the AI suggested actually come from, and that you're allowed to use them.
  • You can explain what it does and why, not just that it appeared to work.
3

If you're not sure

Ask the facilitator during the workshop, or your team's usual channel for security/compliance questions afterward. "I wasn't sure so I asked" is always the right call — it is never the wrong one.
