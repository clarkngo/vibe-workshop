Blast-Radius Audit

Pull five real items from your own backlog and score each one's blast radius and required verification, using Module 03's framework on work that actually matters.

Duration: 25 min Group size: individual, then pairs for the debrief Needs: access to your team's backlog or ticket tracker
1

Recap: the framework

Blast radiusMinimum verification
Low — just for you, easy to undoSkim it, run it, move on.
Medium — others will see or use thisRun it against a real example; check the edge cases you can think of.
High — money, access, personal data, or hard to undoA second person reviews it before it ships. No exceptions.
2

Pull five real items

Open your team's actual backlog — tickets, issues, a TODO list, whatever you really work from. Pick five items you'd genuinely consider tackling with AI assistance. Skip the tempting move of picking five easy ones; a mixed set is where this lab is actually useful.

3

Score each one

4.☐ Low ☐ Med ☐ High
5.☐ Low ☐ Med ☐ High
4

Debrief, in pairs

  • Swap lists. Would your partner have scored any of your items differently? Talk through the disagreement — that's usually where the real judgment call lives.
  • For your one highest-blast-radius item: is that verification step something your team actually does today, or would this be new?
  • Did anything you scored "Low" give you a moment's hesitation? Trust that hesitation — it's usually information.
